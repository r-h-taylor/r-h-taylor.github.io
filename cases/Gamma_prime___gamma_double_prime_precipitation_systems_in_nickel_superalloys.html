Gamma prime / gamma double prime precipitation systems in nickel superalloys

Appears in 1 lecture.

Appearances across the corpus

WM_S2014_11 · Welding Metallurgy, Spring 2014 · §4.p1

The mechanism of strain-age cracking is attributed to rapid precipitation of gamma prime (Ni₃(Al,Ti)) and the cracking susceptibility is reduced by gamma double prime (Ni₃Nb) alloys with lower Ti and Al.

The last weldability issue with nickel-based alloys that I wanted to talk about is strain-age cracking. This is a phenomenon that only happens in the superalloys, or the precipitation-strengthened alloys. It happens because of the rapid precipitation of the strengthening precipitates — the gamma prime precipitate especially. It's similar to reheat cracking in steels.
