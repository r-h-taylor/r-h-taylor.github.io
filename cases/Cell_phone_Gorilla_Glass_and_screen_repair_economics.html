Cell phone Gorilla Glass and screen repair economics

MSE_F2016_10 · Materials Selection, Fall 2016 · §9.p2

Broken cell phone screens cost Americans $23.5 billion to repair or replace from 2007–2014. Corning's Gorilla Glass (alkali-free formulation related to Willow Glass) developed for impact resistance. More than 25% of iPhone users have had a cracked screen.

The last quick thing is cell phones. How many of you have cracked your cell phone? I've definitely done it a few times. You go to the phone company and they get you for $100 or whatever they charge. Broken cell phone screens have cost Americans twenty-three point five billion dollars to repair or replace from 2007 to 2014. An astronomical amount of money. Everyone wants to watch movies on their phone — all cell phones have some type of glass you're looking through. One trade name you'll see often is Gorilla Glass — I believe that's Corning's trademark. They've gone through multiple iterations to make them more impact-resistant, leak-resistant. You've probably seen the commercial with rappers pouring champagne on the glass and they can still use it. There have been quite a few advancements in glass impact resistance for cell phones.
