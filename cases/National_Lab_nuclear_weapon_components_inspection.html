National Lab nuclear weapon components inspection

WM_S2014_16 · Welding Metallurgy, Spring 2014 · §3.p11

Balfour mentions his prior employment at a National Lab making nuclear weapon components, where 100% inspection was required, as a contrasting example to high-volume automotive sampling-based inspection.

For example, I used to work for a National Lab where we made nuclear weapon components, and we inspected every single one of those. If you're making car parts and you're making thousands of them, I'm sure they're not inspecting every single one — they've worked out some kind of statistical system where they're comfortable that if they randomly select and inspect, they're meeting their requirements.
