One of the images here, I believe this was the nose of the plane after the Pensacola, Florida crash. A couple more images — a lot of these images are taken from the NTSB when they're on site to do their accident investigation and reconstruction. What you're seeing here would be a fan hub section that has complete fracture and was released. There was on the news a couple weeks ago, the cover to an engine came off during flight, and the news was showing the engine vibrating back and forth. When something like this happens, a lot of pieces in the engine can be released, and oftentimes they either have to scrap the entire engine, which is millions of dollars loss.
