WM_Su2015_01 · Welding Metallurgy, Summer 2015 ·

Air tubing switched from stainless steel to titanium to save weight for the extended-range 747-400; Engelhard built the catalytic converters for ozone scrubbing in the cabin air system. Introduced here mainly as a vehicle for the aircraft-range discussion (10,000 → 13,000 mile aircraft) rather than as a developed failure case. Tom flags it as a long story not fully told.
