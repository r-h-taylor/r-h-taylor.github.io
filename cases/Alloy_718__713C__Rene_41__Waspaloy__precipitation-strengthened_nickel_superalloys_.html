Alloy 718, 713C, Rene 41, Waspaloy (precipitation-strengthened nickel superalloys)

Appears in 1 lecture.

Appearances across the corpus

WM_S2014_10 · Welding Metallurgy, Spring 2014 · §3.p8

Standard precipitation-strengthened nickel superalloys. Strengthened by gamma prime, gamma double prime, delta phase precipitates.

The next main group is the precipitation strengthened alloys, which includes Alloy 718, 713C. There are Rene alloys — Rene 41 — and Waspaloy alloys. These are all strengthened by precipitates that form during the heat treatment of the alloy. These are called superalloys because of the high strength that can be achieved and the high heat and corrosion resistance that they can withstand.
