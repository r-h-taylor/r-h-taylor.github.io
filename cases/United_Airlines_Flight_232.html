United Airlines Flight 232

Appears in 1 lecture.

Appearances across the corpus

TQI_S2018_03 · Total Quality Improvement, Spring 2018 ·

Centerpiece case study for the lecture. CF6-6 engine stage-one fan rotor failure caused by hard alpha inclusion in the titanium disc, originating in the original ingot. Crack initiated on the virgin flight, grew over many cycles, missed by both ultrasonic and macro-etch inspections at GE manufacturing, and again by fluorescent penetrant inspection 760 cycles before failure (where discoloration around the inclusion was noted but the part was returned to service). Catastrophic disc separation severed all three hydraulic systems; pilot flew the plane manually to Sioux City. 111 fatalities, 47 serious injuries. Disc recovered three months later by a farmer. Case drove FAA process changes in quality assurance and NDE protocols.
