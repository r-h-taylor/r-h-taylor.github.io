Incoloy 925, A-286 (iron-based precipitation hardenable)

WM_S2014_10 · Welding Metallurgy, Spring 2014 · §3.p9

30–35% Ni with higher Fe content; precipitation hardenable.

Some other ones we won't cover much: there are low expansion alloys which contain cobalt. There are solid solution iron-based alloys which still have 30 to 35 percent nickel but have more iron, so they're considered iron-based alloys — those are Incoloy alloys. And then there are precipitation hardenable iron-based alloys as well, such as Incoloy 925 and A-286.
