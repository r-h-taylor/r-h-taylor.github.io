CS_Su2012_06 · Codes and Standards, Summer 2012 · §11.p3

A student wanted to weld spring steel to maximize strength. The lecturer pointed out that welding spring steel destroys its strength. Used as an example of why material/joining decisions must come early in design.

I remember last term we had a student who wanted to build a vessel. First of all he was going to do straight caps — I said no. So it had to be these domes. Then I said, well, looks to me you're going to need some joints. If you're going to use spring steel, thinking you're going to maximize the strength aspect of the metal — well, when you weld spring steel you lose your strength. So it's just going to fall back on you very quickly.
