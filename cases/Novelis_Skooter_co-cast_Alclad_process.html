WM_Su2015_15 · Welding Metallurgy, Summer 2015 · §1.p1

Modern Alclad manufacture. Traditional roll-bonding of pure-aluminum cladding onto a high-strength aluminum-alloy core had ~15% yield. Novelis (Alcan/Péchiney merger entity) developed co-casting that gives ~100% yield. Outside skin of Airbus aircraft is Alclad of this type.
