MSE_F2017_01 · Materials Selection and Economics, Fall 2017 · §2.p4

The next largest is engineered wood product. We've been using wood products for houses and bridges and railroad ties for thousands of years, but one of the problems with wood is nature doesn't always grow the trees in a nice uniform way with homogeneous properties. So now we glue these things together into an engineered product and we don't have to have such big trees. One of the first energy crises of 400 years ago was the fact that they were tearing down all the big trees in England, and they discovered the new world, and one of the first things they did was come over to the new world and start an ironworks right here in Saugus, Massachusetts, because we had big trees. We'll talk about that.
