So it's clear that depending on which industry you're working in, or designing a product for, you'll use a different design approach, and the numerical value of the factor of safety will be different. We'll wrap up today with two more examples. One is an aircraft design example, the other is a roof joist design. They're very different industries; both are governed by codes and standards to a large extent. When I talked about ATVs, when I talked about my trailer, we covered examples of products where the designer or the manufacturer has a lot of flexibility on how they will make the design and how they're going to validate it. Today's lecture is a little bit more prescriptive, and we're going to be looking at two different industries.
