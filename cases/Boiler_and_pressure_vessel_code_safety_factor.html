Boiler and pressure vessel code safety factor

Appears in 1 lecture.

Appearances across the corpus

CS_F2012_07 · Codes and Standards, Fall 2012 · §1.p3

Professor Eagar talked about the pressure vessel code and said you have a safety factor — in this case the safety factor for the thickness of that copper was more than four. This can resist more than 400, 600 psi. And then you're going to have maybe 60 to 80 psi of water pressure from the house, so you have a safety factor of say six to ten. But that wasn't enough. So how much bigger are you really going to make it? When you talk to a plumber and you say you have a risk for freezing, they say, well, we're going to use plastic pipes.
