WRC 1992 diagram (Welding Research Council constitution diagram)

WM_S2014_04 · Welding Metallurgy, Spring 2014 · §8.p8

Balmforth's preferred chart for predicting solidification mode and avoiding cracking. Introduced as a refinement of the Schaeffler diagram with revised chromium and nickel equivalents and explicit solidification-mode boundaries (A, AF, FA, F).

Something that was developed much later than the Schaeffler diagram is this, developed by the Welding Research Council — it's called the WRC 1992 diagram. They modified the chromium and nickel equivalents. Basically this is the region between austenite and ferrite, so it's just a small section of that Schaeffler diagram. They also drew on here the solidification modes. Above this line here you get completely austenitic solidification. You can then see the AF and the FA and the F-type solidification as well on this diagram.
