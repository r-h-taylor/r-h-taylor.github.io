SMS_F2013_08 · Structural Materials Selection, Fall 2013 · §8.p12

Tom's 1990s prediction that we wouldn't be riding around in all-aluminum Ford Tauruses; revisited a decade later as the timeline shifted. Used as the consumer-vehicle case for composites and high-end materials.

Anybody can do it, just like all-aluminum automobiles in the 1930s. You can build a spacecraft out of composites, but can you build a Ford Taurus out of composites? The answer is no. I will predict that we will not be riding around in all-composite Ford Tauruses 20 years from now. 25 years ago I predicted we would not be riding around in all-aluminum Ford Tauruses, at that point 20 years ago. About 10 years later I quit predicting that, because we might be riding around in all-aluminum Ford Tauruses in the next 10 or 15 years. I still don't think we will, but I'm not going to predict it. I don't like to gamble.
