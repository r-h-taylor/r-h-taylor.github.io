Slocum cites the (unnamed) head of Stanley Toolworks R&D who developed the FatMax tool line by listening for customer cuss words rather than satisfaction surveys. Used to support Slocum's "mechanical psychotherapist" framing.

I have to find this article — Popular Mechanics, I think it was. The lady who was the head of Stanley Toolworks R&D, she's the one who came out with the FatMax. Anybody here like Stanley FatMax tools? The journalist's first question was, well, as a woman doing hand tools, a man's carpenter — and she was really good. She said, my dad was in the construction business, I was always on the site, I'm the carpenter.
