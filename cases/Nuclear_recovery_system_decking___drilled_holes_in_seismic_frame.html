Nuclear recovery system decking — drilled holes in seismic frame

Appears in 1 lecture.

Appearances across the corpus

CS_F2012_09 · Codes and Standards, Fall 2012 ·

Dr. Delmar's consulting case. Contractor drilled holes in main structural frame for electrical conduit; frame was designed to deform in plasticity for seismic loading. Plug-welding the holes would have added residual stress and increased crack risk. Resolved with a welded stiffener placed away from the strengthened zone.
