Preloaded bolt connection in heavy crane or bearing application

Appears in 1 lecture.

Appearances across the corpus

CS_Su2012_06 · Codes and Standards, Summer 2012 · §10.p1

Goodman-diagram worked example. Grade 8 bolt, 150 KSI UTS, 20 KSI fatigue endurance, 120 KSI yield. Pre-loading to ~80 KSI mean stress with the flanges acting as a rigid block reduces the bolt's amplitude excursion by ~6–7×, restoring useful safety. Direct tension indicator (DTI) sensors in nuclear-facility erection inspect that pre-load was achieved.

This is going to be really brief, on bolts. A lot of times we specify grade eight if we're going to have a connection where we don't want it to slip — we pre-load the bolts and it's all tight. It's relatively common in advanced structures. The strength of the material is about 150 KSI for steel. This is a different version of a stress-strain analysis for fatigue, so we have stress amplitude and mean stress.
