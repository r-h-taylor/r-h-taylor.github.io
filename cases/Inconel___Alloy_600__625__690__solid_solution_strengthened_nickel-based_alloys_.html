Inconel / Alloy 600, 625, 690 (solid solution strengthened nickel-based alloys)

WM_S2014_10 · Welding Metallurgy, Spring 2014 · §3.p7

Standard solid-solution-strengthened nickel-based alloys, distinguished from precipitation-strengthened group. Bauman notes Inconel name is now post-patent.

So, the standard nickel-based alloys. There's Monel, which is a nickel-copper alloy, Nickel 400. There are two main types of nickel-based alloys we'll be discussing. First is solid solution strengthened alloys. These are strengthened by the alloying elements in them — because of the different size of the different atoms in the solid solution, it puts stress on the lattice and actually strengthens the material. Some that you may hear of are Inconel alloys 600, 625, or 690. They used to be called Inconel; now the patents have run out and they're just called Alloy 600, Alloy 625, etc. Others are Hastelloy X, Haynes 230, RA 330 [333].
