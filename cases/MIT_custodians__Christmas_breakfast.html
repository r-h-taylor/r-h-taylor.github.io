MIT custodians' Christmas breakfast

Appears in 1 lecture.

Appearances across the corpus

WIE_F2015_07 · How to be a Successful Engineer, Fall 2015 · §12.p1

Did I ever tell you about the Christmas breakfast for the custodians when I became department head? When I became department head, I used to get in about six AM. I used to get in earlier than that when I was fighting for tenure, but I finally got a parking space after twenty years, over here by Building Ten, where the custodians would clock out between six and seven. These are the nighttime custodians.
