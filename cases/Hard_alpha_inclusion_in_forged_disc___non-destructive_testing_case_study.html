Treated as the metallurgical/manufacturing root cause of UA 232. Holman uses it to introduce the Type 1 titanium defect taxonomy in §7.p3, then returns to it in §11–§12 as the specific finding of the NTSB report. The macro-etch timing error (performed upstream of the critical machining operations rather than after) is identified as the procedural failure that allowed the inclusion to escape inspection.
