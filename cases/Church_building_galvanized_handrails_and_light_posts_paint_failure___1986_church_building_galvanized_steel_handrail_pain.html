SMS_F2014_06 · Structural Materials Selection, Fall 2014 · §10.p1

Forensic case as personal narrative. Galvanized steel painted before zinc hydroxide formation specification observed → paint failed within a year. Acetic acid pre-treatment (hypothesized zinc acetate formation) → repaint lasted thirty years. Used to teach surface-chemistry preparation for paint adhesion on galvanized substrates.
