Tom's first consulting contract after returning to MIT, before the Sylvester engagement. Mentioned in passing. ## Cases referenced in passing

I had one consulting contract with the gold company down south of here in Attleboro, but I was still looking for a little more. A guy down south of here, Bruce Sylvester — his father had worked for Factory Mutual as a metallurgist and then had left Factory Mutual Research to start his own consulting firm, which did non-destructive testing. His chemist was the guy who used to do chemical analyses when the Navy had a Navy shipyard here in Boston, and when they closed the shipyard, that guy went to work for Sylvester, at JG Sylvester and Associates.
