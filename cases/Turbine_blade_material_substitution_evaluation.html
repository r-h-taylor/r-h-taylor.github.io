Turbine blade material substitution evaluation

Appears in 1 lecture.

Appearances across the corpus

CS_Su2012_06 · Codes and Standards, Summer 2012 · §9.p7

Substitution candidate evaluated against original alloy. Limited fatigue data (weeks per data point) means the 95% confidence interval consumes most of the apparent margin: at 10⁷ cycles, the nominal life of 25 collapses to 17–18 inside the confidence band. Teaches: confidence intervals are not safety factor; you cannot spend the same margin twice.

In reality, we have experimental data points. This is very different — the stress levels are higher, we're looking at some turbine blade data. A superalloy type material, so it's got a much higher stress level than what we looked at for the aluminum. This was a situation where they were trying to evaluate if they could change the alloy from one to the other. Given everything we talked about, to get good reliability you need your field experience, you need your testing, large-scale testing, you need calculation — you need all of that. When you change material, to make sure you don't affect your factor of safety, you try to compare them with respect to every single property, or at least every property that matters for your design.
