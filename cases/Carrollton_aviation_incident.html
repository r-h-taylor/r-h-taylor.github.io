Named in the cost-benefit four-accident comparison; no further detail.

If we look at the four incidents in that twenty-year period, Sioux City Iowa is by far the most catastrophic of them all, compared to Carrollton flight, ValuJet, and the Pensacola crash. 111 fatalities, 47 serious injuries. Taking into account all the costs associated with injury, loss of the aircraft, engines, and things like that, this number trumps far and away — it was over eighty percent of all the costs associated in that twenty-year period. And those four were due to non-destructive testing issues. There are a lot of other events that weren't included in this.
