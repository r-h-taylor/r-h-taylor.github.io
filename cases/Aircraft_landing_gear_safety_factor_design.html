Aircraft landing gear safety factor design

CS_F2012_07 · Codes and Standards, Fall 2012 · §5.p4

Used as a counter-intuitive example — landing gear has a 1.2 safety factor (lower than building piping at 1.5–2), but is more reliable, because the load is well-determined, the material is forged and weld-free, and quality control is high.

Aircraft structures — I put a 1.5 safety factor on it because that's the number that kept coming back. I worked in the airframe business for a little bit, and there's a range. The landing gear is designed with a safety factor of about 1.2. There are reasons for that. The main reason is, it's heavy. And it's critical. And if you think about those two aspects: you can determine the load. There's only so fast you can run into the ground. And you can only break so fast.
