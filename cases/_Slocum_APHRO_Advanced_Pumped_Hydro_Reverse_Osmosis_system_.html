WIE_F2015_12 · How to be a Successful Engineer, Fall 2015 · §5.p1

Slocum's signature post-2001 project. The physics coincidence: optimal hydroelectric head height (500 m, 50 atm) equals optimal reverse-osmosis membrane pressure. Therefore coastal-mountain sites can deliver electricity and desalinated fresh water from the same infrastructure. Sites identified worldwide; main work in Mexico, Iran (highest A-index), UAE (Masdar).

So I asked, what are you going to do with the next $2 trillion? What I said back then was: if you take $4 per installed watt of renewables, solar and wind — and that's a lot of money, $4 — you could have 500 gigawatts of CO2-free 24/7 electric power. That's actually the instantaneous average consumption in the United States. We could have said to the rest of the world: we don't know what we did to piss you off, we're not admitting anything, but instead of spending the next ten years and $2 trillion on war, we're going to make you irrelevant. We're going to sell that technology all over the world to make oil irrelevant everywhere. That really scared people off. Nothing makes a person more scared than being irrelevant.
