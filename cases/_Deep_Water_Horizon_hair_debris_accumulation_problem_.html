`Deep Water Horizon hair/debris accumulation problem`

WIE_F2015_12 · How to be a Successful Engineer, Fall 2015 · §5.p4

Slocum's Houston-based startup. He served on Secretary Chu's Deepwater Horizon team. The "hairball generator" is a device for sending wire into a blowout preventer to deliberately create a debris ball — Slocum's tongue-in-cheek inversion of the actual failure mode at Macondo.

Then there's the Britney effect — oops, it's going to happen again. I had a doctoral student — we have a neat new company. I was a member of Secretary Chu's team on the Deepwater Horizon. I have three sons but also three nieces, and what happens when they leave my house: they take showers, and long hair comes out. We designed a machine to send wire into a blowout preventer and create a hairball. We didn't do it for the Deepwater Horizon, that's another long story. It's a new company in Houston, got great funding, run by a former doctoral student. It's the Hairball Generator. It will happen again — but this time, you're going to fix it right away.
