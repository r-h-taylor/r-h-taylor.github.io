Welder recertification burden in oil and construction (early 1990s)

Appears in 1 lecture.

Appearances across the corpus

WM_Su2014_19 · Welding Quality, Summer 2014 · §7.p4

Used to motivate the early-1990s push for portable, employer-independent AWS welder certification — oil field welders changing companies every six months and paying $500 each time.

What happened was, in the early 90s, people said, it's good to have some pre-qualified procedures, but we need more. We need to simplify this certification. We want to provide a certification so an individual who wants to qualify doesn't have to re-qualify when he changes companies. It used to be, if you were working for Caterpillar in Peoria, and you had to move to Iowa and you got a job with John Deere, you had to be retested at a cost of about five hundred dollars, because you had to be qualified by your employer.
