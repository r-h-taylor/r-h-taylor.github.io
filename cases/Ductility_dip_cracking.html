The three main weldability issues in nickel-based alloys are solidification cracking, heat affected zone liquation cracking, and strain age cracking. Strain age cracking is similar to reheat cracking that I talked about a little bit with martensitic stainless steels and also in other steels. There's also something called ductility dip cracking, which I'm not going to talk about — if you're interested in that, you can look in the book, or you can get in touch with me.
