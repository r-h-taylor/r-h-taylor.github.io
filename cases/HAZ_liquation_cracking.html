The next weldability issue is heat affected zone — HAZ — liquation cracking. The heat affected zone is the region of material next to the material from the weld that was molten or liquid — so it was not melted, but it was definitely affected by the heat of the weld. The reason HAZ cracks can occur is that alloys melt over a range of temperatures. If you remember from the phase diagrams Professor Eagar has shown you, and the stainless steel phase diagrams I showed you, as the temperature drops in an alloy, you go from liquid to a liquid plus solid region, and then to a completely solid region.
