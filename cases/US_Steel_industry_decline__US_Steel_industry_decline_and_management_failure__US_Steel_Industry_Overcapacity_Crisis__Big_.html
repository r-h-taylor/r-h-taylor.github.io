MSE_F2017_01 · Materials Selection and Economics, Fall 2017 · §7.p4

But we tolerated a lot of different things in the old days. There are companies today that have serious problems because of what they call legacy costs. The steel companies produce millions of tons of steel and have for more than a hundred years, and they used to take their slag and other things and just make landfills. That was acceptable when they did it, but it's not acceptable today. One of the reasons USX cannot make very much money in the steel business is because they have these legacy costs. A lot of companies go out of business because they can't afford the legacy costs of cleaning up what was legal fifty years before. You have to look at not just what the costs are of doing business today, but what the costs are in the future as well.
