CS_Su2012_01 · Codes and Standards, Summer 2012 · §6.p5

In order to run this thing, they had to align the mirror at one end with the mirror at the other within 1 cm over 4 kilometers. One centimeter over 4 km is one part in 400,000. That's pretty precise. No one had ever needed to survey 1 cm over 4 km before. They had to develop new surveying techniques using GPS. Anybody know what wave GPS is? I've heard of it. Wave GPS is, if you build a little ground transmitter to help synchronize all the satellites up there in space, you can get a factor of 10 or 100 more accurate. You use the satellites up there in space, but you have a reference point here that helps calibrate all of them to a reference position. They used wave GPS to do the civil engineering surveying, because no civil engineers had ever done anything this precise before.
